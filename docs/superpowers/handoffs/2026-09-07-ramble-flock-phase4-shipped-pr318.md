## Rulings to carry
- `renderAr` accepts an optional fourth field `camera` (default true); the mode/reason decision lives in the renderer. Reason priority: `no-fix` > `no-camera` > `no-heading`.
- The bird's AR line is a navigation line, not the perch mood line (spec amendment).
- Coarse anchors (error radius > `COARSE_M` 150 m) get no direction; 7-char teasers (~101 m) are dashed directional labels.
- Contacts marks: retention (50), not per-day — `created_at` is the sender's clock. Caws published at precision ≤ 4 are on the map but not in AR (stated limitation).
- Hidden tab: camera off only; the view stays open.
- `/around` lists what the map lists (public, contacts, own private).
- `panel/routes.js` hard-requires `server/around.js`: an installed copy that did not refresh to 0.5.0 fails EVERY `/api/ramble/*` route — the refresh line in the journal is load-bearing on each gateway.
